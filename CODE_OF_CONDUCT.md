# Code of Conduct

Maritime Telemetry welcomes people of every experience level and background. Help
make this a respectful place to learn, build and improve maritime interoperability.
This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other project
spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient and constructive. Critique ideas and evidence without
  attacking the person presenting them.
- Welcome newcomers and different viewpoints. Ask clarifying questions and accept
  corrections with care.
- Respect people's identity, privacy, time and boundaries.
- Share technical claims honestly, including uncertainty and limitations.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, repeated disruption and retaliation for a good-faith report are unacceptable.
Stop when someone asks you to stop unwanted contact or behavior.

## Reporting a concern

Email [magnus919@pm.me](mailto:magnus919@pm.me) to report a conduct concern privately.
Include the relevant context and links or evidence you are comfortable sharing.
Do not post incident details or identify the people involved in a public issue.

You can also use GitHub's **Report abuse** option for content or behavior that
violates its platform rules, including when a concern involves a project maintainer.
That report goes to GitHub, which handles it under its own policies; it is separate
from project moderation. See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

For software vulnerabilities, use the separate private route in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation and enforcement

Maintainers may request a change in behavior, edit or remove contributions, limit
participation, or temporarily or permanently exclude someone from project spaces.
They will consider the context, severity and pattern of behavior and give an
explanation where practical. Reports and private evidence should be shared only
with those needed to review and respond to the concern.

To ask for a moderation decision to be reconsidered, email the same contact and
provide relevant context. Maintainers are expected to follow this code too. No
response deadline or absolute confidentiality is promised.
